## Product Principles

- Define each product surface by the decision or task it helps a user complete.
- Give every displayed metric an explicit meaning, source, unit, time window, and freshness rule.
- Distinguish source data, transformed data, and inferred or generated content.
- Design loading, empty, partial-data, stale-data, and error states alongside the successful state.
- Make important information keyboard-accessible, responsive, and understandable without relying on color alone.
- Keep private or sensitive data within confirmed storage and sharing boundaries.

## Open Product Decisions

- Who are the primary users, and what decisions or tasks should the product support?
- Which data sources are authoritative, and how current must they be?
- Which views, filters, comparisons, alerts, and exports are needed?
- What authentication, authorization, and privacy rules apply?
- Which delivery environment and operational constraints apply?
