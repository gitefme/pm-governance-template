# Product Design Brief

## Current Status

This template contains no confirmed product scope. Replace this placeholder with the new project's intended users, outcomes, workflows, constraints, and open decisions. Do not treat examples or implementation suggestions as product decisions.

## Product Principles

- Define each product surface by the decision or task it helps a user complete.
- Give every displayed metric an explicit meaning, source, unit, time window, and freshness rule.
- Distinguish source data, transformed data, and inferred or generated content.
- Design loading, empty, partial-data, stale-data, and error states alongside the successful state.
- Make important information keyboard-accessible, responsive, and understandable without relying on color alone.
- Keep private or sensitive data within confirmed storage and sharing boundaries.

## Open Product Decisions

- Who are the primary users, and what decisions or tasks should the product support?
- Which data sources are authoritative, and how current must they be?
- Which views, filters, comparisons, alerts, and exports are needed?
- What authentication, authorization, and privacy rules apply?
- Which delivery environment and operational constraints apply?

Record confirmed answers here. Convert implementation work into formal tasks in `BACKLOG.md`; keep raw possibilities in `IDEA_INBOX.md`.

## Authority and Evidence

Confirmed target rules describe intended behavior, not proof that it exists. Keep implementation status, dated verification, and outstanding acceptance in task records and linked evidence. Under explicit write-first authorization, isolate proposed sections with Formulation Status: proposed, Storage Authorization, and visible open decisions. They do not override confirmed rules. Remove the proposed marker only after confirmation; record the decision and affected revision. A plan's approval does not silently approve unrelated changes to this document.
